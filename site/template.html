The pop goes to the offer price

Institutions get most of the shares at the offer price, and they capture the first-day jump. Most retail buyers get in after that, and from there the typical result is close to a coin flip.

SpaceX fits the pattern. Anyone who got shares at $135 is still up. Anyone who bought during the first week's run-up is down.

Matching the market cycle

For each IPO we measured how the Nasdaq had done in the 3 and 12 months before it priced. A hotter market goes with a bigger first-day pop (correlation about 0.3–0.4), but it tells you almost nothing about the year after.

Right now the Nasdaq is up about 6% over 3 months and 12% over 12 months. That is a moderate market, closer to 2019 than to the 2020–21 frenzy. The seven IPOs priced into the most similar markets are listed here.

Getting shares at the offer price

The numbers above say the advantage is in getting shares at the offer price. For SpaceX, UK retail investors could apply through Interactive Brokers UK, Hargreaves Lansdown and AJ Bell during a six-day window (4–10 June) that closed just before pricing. Allocations were not guaranteed and were scaled back when demand was high.

As of 27 October no broker has confirmed retail access to ANT. If it follows the SpaceX pattern, the application window will open around when the price range is published, so we're watching broker announcements from week 2.

The six-week plan

The project runs from 27 October to 8 December. Anthropic's schedule drives the dates, so they may shift.

  1. Week 1 (to 2 Nov): remove the survivor bias. Replace our hand-picked list with every US IPO over $1B since 2000, using Jay Ritter's IPO dataset and SEC filings.
  2. Week 2 (3–9 Nov): model it. Regress the day-1 and 6-month returns on the price-range revision, price-to-sales at the offer, deal size and market conditions. Watch which brokers announce retail access.
  3. Week 3 (10–16 Nov): make a call. When the price range is published, plug in Anthropic's numbers and post a forecast with a range before the shares price.
  4. Week 4 (17–23 Nov): pricing and day one. Score our day-1 forecast against what actually happens.
  5. Weeks 5–6 (24 Nov–8 Dec): the first weeks of trading. Track ANT against the median path, then write up and present at Project Labs.
